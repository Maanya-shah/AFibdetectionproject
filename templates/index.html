Feature Reference Guide

RMSSD — Root mean square of successive beat interval differences. High RMSSD means beats are arriving very irregularly — classic AFib sign.
pNN50 — Percentage of consecutive beat intervals differing by >50ms. Normal hearts have low pNN50. AFib patients typically show >20%.
SD1 (Poincaré) — Short-term beat-to-beat variability. Elevated SD1 indicates rapid random changes in rhythm typical of AFib.
Entropy — Unpredictability of the beat interval sequence. AFib produces high entropy (chaotic). Normal sinus rhythm is more predictable.
Mean RR — Average time between heartbeats (ms). Normal resting value ~600–1000ms. Indicates overall heart rate.
SD2 (Poincaré) — Long-term beat variability. Reflects slower autonomic nervous system control of heart rhythm.
LF/HF Ratio — Low-frequency to high-frequency power ratio. Reflects autonomic balance. Disrupted in AFib due to loss of vagal control.
Heart Rate — Beats per minute derived from R-peak spacing. AFib often presents with elevated and irregular ventricular rate.
